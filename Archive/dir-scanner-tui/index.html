Directory Scanner TUI

Ratatui app that scans a directory tree, shows system info, top files/dirs, and saves a report to ~/Developer/macos-reset/.

just dir-scanner-tui
just dir-scanner-tui "$HOME/Developer"
SCAN_ROOT="$HOME" just dir-scanner-tui

Keys: Tab / 1–7 switch panels, j/k scroll, s save, q quit.
